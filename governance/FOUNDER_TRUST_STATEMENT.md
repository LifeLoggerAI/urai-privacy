# Founder Trust Statement

I did not build URAI to extract value from people’s lives.

I built it because modern systems flatten human experience into metrics,
and people deserve something better — something that reflects meaning,
memory, and growth without turning vulnerability into a commodity.

URAI will never trade trust for growth.
If the only way forward requires crossing ethical lines,
then that path is not forward at all.

The responsibility for this system — its limits, its failures,
and its impact — ultimately rests with me.

That responsibility is not abstract.
It is personal.

— Adam Clamp  
Founder, URAI
