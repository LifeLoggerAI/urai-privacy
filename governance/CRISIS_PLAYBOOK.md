# URAI CRISIS & HOSTILE SCENARIO PLAYBOOK (v1.0)

## Purpose

This document defines how URAI responds to hostile events without panic, deflection, or erosion of trust.

Hostile events include:

*   Media attacks
*   Viral misinformation
*   Regulator inquiries
*   Legal threats
*   Security incidents
*   Public fear cascades

The goal is stability, clarity, and restraint.

## 1️⃣ Core Rules (Never Break These)

*   Do not rush statements
*   Do not speculate
*   Do not argue online
*   Do not shift blame to “the model”
*   Do not hide policy pages
*   Do not silently change terms

Silence is acceptable. Confusion is not.

## 2️⃣ Scenario A — Viral Fear Post

“This app spies on your emotions” / “AI therapy scam” / “Surveillance app”

**Immediate Action (0–6 hours)**

*   Do nothing publicly
*   Capture screenshots
*   Identify the exact claim
*   Verify if claim contradicts published policy

**Official Response (if needed)**

Respond once, link only:

> URAI does not sell personal data, does not diagnose users, and does not train AI on user data by default.
> Our policies are public here: https://urai.app/trust

No follow-ups. No threads.

## 3️⃣ Scenario B — Journalist Inquiry

**First Response Template**

> Thanks for reaching out.
>
> URAI publishes its privacy, trust, and AI boundaries publicly.
> Those documents represent our official position.
>
> If you have a specific factual question not addressed there,
> we’re happy to clarify.

Never answer hypothetical traps.

## 4️⃣ Scenario C — Regulator Inquiry

**Immediate Steps**

*   Pause all policy changes
*   Preserve logs
*   Acknowledge receipt
*   Provide the PDF packet

**Language**

> URAI is committed to transparency and compliance.
> Please see the attached Trust & Privacy Packet (v1.0.0).

Never over-explain.

## 5️⃣ Scenario D — Law Enforcement Request

Handled only via `/law-enforcement` process.

*   No verbal disclosures
*   No informal cooperation
*   No scope expansion

Everything documented.

## 6️⃣ Scenario E — Security Incident

**Timeline**

*   T+0: isolate
*   T+24h: assess
*   T+72h: disclose if required

**Disclosure Principles**

*   Facts only
*   No minimization
*   No speculation
*   Clear remediation steps

Trust is preserved by honesty, not spin.

## 7️⃣ Scenario F — “Is This Therapy?” Attack

**Canonical Answer**

> URAI is not therapy and does not provide medical or diagnostic services.
> It is a reflective system designed to surface patterns, not prescribe outcomes.

This answer never changes.

## 8️⃣ Scenario G — Founder Pressure

If pressure is placed on you personally to:

*   “Just tweak the language”
*   “Temporarily relax boundaries”
*   “Monetize insights quietly”

Stop.

That is the exact moment this document exists for.

## 9️⃣ Authority Chain

Final authority on trust decisions:

*   Published policies
*   `LOCK.md`
*   Founder Trust Statement

No ad hoc exceptions.

## 🔟 Completion Signal

This playbook is never announced.
It exists so you never improvise under pressure.
