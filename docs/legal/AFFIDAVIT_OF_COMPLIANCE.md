**System Identification**

The repository operates under an active governance lock (governance/LOCK.md) which prohibits weakening of user privacy rights, sale of personal data, or removal of auditability. Said lock remains unmodified.

Consent is explicitly scoped, versioned, revocable, and enforced. Revocation results in immediate denial of further processing.

All sensitive actions are recorded in immutable audit logs, including actor identity, timestamp, action performed, and outcome.

**Transparency**

Policies are publicly accessible, versioned, and historically preserved. User acceptance is recorded per version.

**No Sale of Personal Data**

URAI does not sell personal data. This principle is immutable under governance lock.
