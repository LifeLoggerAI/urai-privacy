# URAI Product Boundary

URAI product copy may use this launch promise:

```text
Private by default. User-owned by design. No ads inside URAI.
```

## Product experience boundary

The no-ads promise applies to private URAI product surfaces, including Home Field, Orb Companion, Memory Galaxy, Symbolic Replay, Council, Passport, Privacy Center, and related Life OS experiences.

Private product surfaces should not contain third-party ad placements or use private product data for third-party ad targeting.

## Public website boundary

Public websites may use limited analytics, cookie consent tooling, and campaign attribution where allowed and disclosed.

Website measurement should remain separate from private product data and should not silently expand into additional tracking, profiling, sharing, or monetization beyond the user's consent.

## Passport and Privacy Center boundary

Users should be able to review, change, export, delete, or revoke applicable product permissions through Passport or the Privacy Center.

Any future marketplace, licensing, or external personalization layer should be opt-in, revocable, clearly explained, and separated from the private Genesis experience.

## Release gate

If a feature touches advertising, website measurement, data sharing, marketplace participation, or external personalization, it must pass privacy review before release.
