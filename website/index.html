Human-readable controls

CONSENT

What did I agree to?

Consent is granular, purpose-bound, revocable where applicable, and separate from vague acceptance of a whole ecosystem.

ACCESS

Which systems can use it?

Authorized systems should be named by role and purpose instead of hidden behind a generic “partners” label.

RETENTION

How long does it remain?

Retention classes and deletion behavior are explicit enough to explain what ends immediately and what requires durable processing.

EXPORT

Can I take my information with me?

Export is treated as a real product right with request, processing, completion, expiration, and failure states.

DELETE

What disappears if I delete?

Destructive flows explain scope, downstream consequences, irreversible boundaries, and any legally required retained records before confirmation.

EVIDENCE

How do I know the control worked?

Privacy-relevant actions produce inspectable status and audit evidence rather than disappearing behind a success toast.

Permission classes

Automation changes its behavior as consequence rises.

SILENT_AUTOLow-risk processing already covered by an explicit valid grant. Activity remains inspectable where appropriate.
REVERSIBLE_AUTOAutomation may proceed only with a clear undo, cancellation, or rollback path.
ASK_FIRSTThe action waits for explicit confirmation with purpose and consequence explained.
STRONG_CONFIRMATIONConsequential action requires higher-friction confirmation and a clear description of what cannot be undone.
NEVER_AUTONOMOUSURAI may prepare or explain, but execution remains with the authorized human.
Rights and enforcement

Control has to survive the happy path.

Revoked grants, delayed exports, deletion failures, provider outages, stale evidence, and policy blocks are privacy states—not generic errors. The governance package defines the rules; product surfaces must expose truthful status without silently escalating access.
