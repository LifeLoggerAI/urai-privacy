Principles

Privacy is not a feature. It is the operating system.

URAI Privacy defines the minimum rules every URAI app, model, admin tool, analytics job, data-sharing process, and public notice must obey.

Consent first

Sensitive inference, biometrics, location, personalization, and data-sharing must use explicit, granular, revocable consent.

Data minimization

Collect less, infer less, retain less, and prefer on-device processing when feasible.

No silent escalation

URAI must not quietly expand collection, inference, retention, sharing, or monetization beyond the user's expectation.

User sovereignty

Users need real export, deletion, revocation, explanation, and privacy-history controls.

Enforceable policy

Privacy rules are machine-readable and validated by CI, not left only in prose.

Higher care for sensitive data

Biometrics, mental health, location, minors, crisis signals, and data-sharing require stronger review.

Default decision bias

When a privacy decision is uncertain, URAI chooses the safer path: collect less, retain less, infer less, share less, explain more, and ask for clearer consent.
