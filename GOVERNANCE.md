# URAI Privacy Governance Conduct

URAI Privacy exists to protect users, enforce consent, and prevent silent escalation of data collection, inference, retention, sharing, or monetization.

## Governance Principles

Contributors should act with:

- user sovereignty
- privacy-by-architecture
- data minimization
- transparency
- accuracy
- accountability
- respect for sensitive contexts
- caution around biometrics, mental health, location, minors, and data-sharing

## Decision Bias

When uncertain, choose the safer privacy-preserving path:

- collect less
- retain less
- infer less
- share less
- explain more
- ask for clearer consent
- give users stronger control

## Prohibited Governance Patterns

- dark patterns
- bundled consent for sensitive categories
- silent escalation
- vague purposes
- unreviewed sensitive inference
- unreviewed biometric processing
- data-sharing without C8 consent
- admin curiosity access
- weak or missing deletion/export support

## Review Culture

Privacy review should be specific, evidence-based, and oriented toward making the system safer and easier for users to understand.
