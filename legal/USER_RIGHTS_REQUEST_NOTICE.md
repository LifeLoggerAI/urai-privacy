# User Rights Request Notice Template

> This template is not legal advice. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel before production use.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and the URAI features you use, you may be able to request access, export, deletion, correction, restriction, opt-out, or human review of certain data and AI-generated insights.

## Available Request Types

### Data Export

Request a copy of your URAI data, including applicable account data, consent history, memories, transcripts, insights, privacy request history, and data-sharing participation records.

### Deletion

Request deletion of individual records, categories of data, date ranges, biometric-derived data, or your account where applicable.

### Consent Revocation

Revoke non-essential consent tiers, including sensitive AI inference, biometric identity processing, location context, personalization, or anonymized data-sharing participation.

### Correction

Request correction of inaccurate account information or user-controlled records where supported.

### Human Review

Request human review of a privacy-sensitive issue, sensitive AI inference, deletion/export failure, or data-sharing concern.

## Authentication

URAI may require authentication or step-up verification before fulfilling sensitive requests.

## Timing

URAI should acknowledge privacy requests promptly and complete them within applicable legal timelines. If a request cannot be completed, URAI should explain why.
