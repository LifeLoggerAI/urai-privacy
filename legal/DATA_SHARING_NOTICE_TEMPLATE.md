# Data-Sharing and Anonymized Data Participation Notice Template

> This template is not legal advice. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel before production use.

## What This Notice Covers

This notice explains optional anonymized data-sharing or data participation programs in URAI.

## Separate Opt-In Required

URAI data-sharing participation must be off by default and requires separate, explicit opt-in consent. It must not be bundled with essential account access, memory storage, sensitive AI inference, or biometric identity consent.

## What May Be Shared

Only approved anonymized or aggregate data products may be shared. These may include cohort-level behavioral, wellness, product usage, or pattern summaries when re-identification risk is controlled.

## What Must Not Be Shared

URAI must not share, sell, license, or transfer:

- raw audio
- raw transcripts
- raw images
- raw biometric templates
- voiceprints
- face embeddings
- user-linked sensitive inference records
- individual location trails
- small-cohort sensitive patterns

## Cohort and Re-Identification Controls

Data-sharing products must meet the minimum cohort threshold defined by URAI Privacy governance and must suppress, generalize, or remove rare combinations that increase re-identification risk.

## Opt-Out

Users must be able to opt out of future participation. Opt-out stops future inclusion in unreleased or pending data-sharing batches.

## Revenue / Participation Ledger

If URAI offers user participation, revenue, or benefit tracking, the system should maintain a user-visible ledger showing participation status, opt-in/opt-out events, and applicable data product categories.
