# Cookie and Local Storage Notice Template

> This template is not legal advice. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel before production use.

## What This Notice Covers

URAI may use cookies, local storage, device storage, or similar technologies to operate the app, keep users signed in, remember privacy preferences, support security, and improve reliability.

## Categories

### Essential Storage

Used for authentication, security, session continuity, privacy preferences, and service operation.

### Preference Storage

Used to remember user settings, accessibility preferences, consent choices, theme settings, and product configuration.

### Analytics Storage

Used only where consented or legally permitted to understand aggregate app performance, reliability, and product usage.

### Sensitive Feature Storage

Any local storage related to sensitive inference, biometric processing, location, or AI personalization must follow the active URAI Privacy governance version.

## No Silent Escalation

URAI must not use cookies or local storage to silently expand tracking, profiling, inference, advertising, or data-sharing beyond the user's consent.

## User Controls

Users should be able to manage non-essential storage choices, revoke applicable consent, and understand how stored preferences affect the product.
