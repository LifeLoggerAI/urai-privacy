# Children and Minors Privacy Policy Template

> This template is not legal advice. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel before any child-directed or minor-related launch.

## Current Position

URAI should not knowingly collect, infer, store, or process personal data from children or minors unless a dedicated minor privacy program has been reviewed and approved.

## Higher-Risk Data Categories

For minors, the following categories require special review and stronger default restrictions:

- audio and transcripts
- location context
- sensitive AI inference
- mental health or crisis indicators
- relationship and social graph signals
- biometric or identity signals
- data-sharing or monetization participation

## Default Restrictions

Unless legally reviewed and explicitly approved, URAI should not:

- process minor biometric identity signals
- include minors in anonymized data-sharing products
- create sensitive mental health, crisis, trauma, or deception inferences about minors
- retain raw audio, images, or transcripts from minors beyond the shortest feasible period
- use minor data for advertising, profiling, or commercial data products

## Consent and Guardian Controls

Where legally required, URAI must support parental or guardian consent, age-appropriate disclosures, revocation, deletion, and data access controls.

## Age Assurance

Any age assurance flow must be proportionate, privacy-preserving, and avoid collecting more data than necessary.

## Launch Requirement

Any feature intended for minors, likely to be used by minors, or processing minor data requires a dedicated DPIA, legal review, and launch readiness approval.
