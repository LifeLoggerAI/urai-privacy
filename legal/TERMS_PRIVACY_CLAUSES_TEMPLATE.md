# Terms of Service Privacy Clauses Template

> This template is not legal advice. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel before production use.

## Privacy Governance

URAI operates under a privacy governance framework that defines how data may be collected, inferred, stored, exported, deleted, audited, and shared.

## User Consent

Some URAI features require explicit user consent, including sensitive AI inference, biometric identity processing, location context, personalization, and anonymized data-sharing participation.

## User Control

Users may be able to:

- revoke non-essential consent
- request export of their data
- request deletion of records or account data
- request deletion of biometric-derived templates where applicable
- opt out of future anonymized data-sharing participation
- request explanations for sensitive insights

## Sensitive AI Inference

URAI may provide optional insights about mood, mental load, relationship patterns, behavioral rhythms, or other sensitive personal patterns. These features should be clearly disclosed and consented to before use.

## Data-Sharing Restrictions

URAI must not sell raw biometric identifiers, raw transcripts, raw personal content, or user-linked sensitive inference records.

Any anonymized data-sharing or monetization program must use separate opt-in consent, cohort protections, and audit logging.

## Legal Review Requirement

These clauses should be synchronized with the current privacy policy, biometric/AI inference notice, and data-sharing notice before release.
