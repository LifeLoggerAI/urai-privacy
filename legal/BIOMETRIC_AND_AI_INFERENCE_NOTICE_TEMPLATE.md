# Biometric and AI Inference Notice Template

> This template must be reviewed by qualified counsel before public launch.

## Optional Biometric Features

URAI may offer optional features that use voice, face, gaze, or other identity-related signals. These features are off unless you choose to enable them.

Examples may include:

- recognizing familiar voices
- matching a speaker to a saved voice profile
- estimating facial expression or gaze direction
- creating private, on-device identity vectors

## What We Do Not Do

URAI must not sell raw biometric identifiers, raw voiceprints, raw face embeddings, or identity vectors.

URAI must not require biometric enrollment for essential app access unless a specific security feature needs it and a non-biometric alternative is available where required.

## Storage and Deletion

Biometric-derived templates should be stored for the shortest feasible period and should be deletable independently from the rest of the account.

## Sensitive AI Inferences

URAI may generate optional insights about mood, mental load, relationship dynamics, behavioral rhythms, or personal patterns. These insights are sensitive and require explicit consent.

## User Controls

Users should be able to:

- enable or disable biometric features
- delete biometric templates
- disable sensitive AI insights
- export relevant privacy history
- see an explanation for why an insight was generated
